# Price data

## What is in this folder

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `staples.csv` | Our hand-picked list of 86 student staples: English and Spanish name, category, diet tag, allergen tags, and an average `piece_grams` for produce that people count rather than weigh (one banana, two tomatoes). Written by us. |
| `skus_mercadona.csv`, `skus_dia.csv` | For each staple, the product (SKU) we chose in that shop. An empty SKU means the shop does not sell it online (Dia: tofu). The `note` column records substitutions (Dia: fresh salmon loins instead of frozen portions, processed sandwich cheese instead of natural slices). |
| `prices_mercadona_mad1_2026-09-21.csv`, `prices_dia_es-default_2026-09-21.csv` | The price snapshots the app uses, one per supermarket. Generated by `scripts/build_price_snapshot.py`. Do not edit by hand. |
| `raw/` | Downloaded source files. Ignored by Git. |

## Where the prices come from

Prices are **real online prices captured on 2026-09-21**: Mercadona for the Madrid zone (`mad1`) and Dia for its national online shop (`es-default`).

Source: [OpenCesta](https://github.com/ruvelro/opencesta), an open dataset of Spanish supermarket prices, release `prices-2026-09-21`. The dataset is published under the [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) licence. Our snapshots are filtered extracts of it and are shared under the same licence.

We use fixed snapshots on purpose. No supermarket has an official public API, and scraping their websites would be fragile and against the spirit of their terms. A dated snapshot keeps the app reproducible: the same inputs give the same shopping total.

## How the two shops differ

- Mercadona sells loose fruit and vegetables by the piece (one potato = 0.26 kg). Dia sells most of them in bags or trays (potatoes 1 kg, tomatoes 500 g). The app handles both because every quantity is converted to grams before pricing.
- Dia's data has no package size column, only a price per kg / litre / unit. The build script derives the size from it (0.55 EUR at 1.38 EUR/kg is a 400 g can). Eggs and lettuce are given explicitly in `skus_dia.csv`.
- Same ingredient ids everywhere, so one meal plan can be priced in every shop.

## Limitations (read before trusting a number)

- Prices are frozen on 2026-09-21. They will drift from shelf prices over time.
- Mercadona prices are for the Madrid online zone; Dia prices are its national online prices. Shops in other cities can differ.
- One product per ingredient, usually the cheapest store-brand option. The app cannot compare brands.
- Piece weights (`piece_grams`) are averages we chose, not measured.
- **Allergen and diet tags were written by us from general knowledge, not read from product labels.** They are indicative only. Always check the real label.

## How to rebuild the snapshots

```
pip install -r scripts/requirements.txt
mkdir -p data/raw
curl -L -o data/raw/prices.tar.gz https://github.com/ruvelro/opencesta/releases/download/prices-2026-09-21/prices.tar.gz
tar -xzf data/raw/prices.tar.gz -C data/raw
python3 scripts/build_price_snapshot.py "data/raw/data/chain=mercadona/zone=mad1/date=2026-09-21/prices.parquet"
python3 scripts/build_price_snapshot.py "data/raw/data/chain=dia/zone=es-default/date=2026-09-21/prices.parquet"
```

The script stops with an error if any SKU from the mapping file is missing from the price file.

## Adding a third supermarket

The release also contains Ahorramás (Madrid). Create `data/skus_ahorramas.csv` with the same columns, run the script on its Parquet file, and the new shop appears on the landing page and in the comparison without any code change.
