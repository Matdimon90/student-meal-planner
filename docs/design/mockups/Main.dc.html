One week, fourteen ingredients, nothing thrown away.

Every strand is an ingredient. It opens on the day you first cook it, runs through every day it's reused, and either ends clean or trails off as a leftover. This is what your plan will look like.

How it works

Five steps. Two are AI, three are plain code.

  1. 1
    You set the budget, people, days and what you already have.
    You
  2. 2
    The model drafts meals that reuse ingredients across days.
    It writes the recipes. It never sees or invents a price.
    AI
  3. 3
    Each ingredient is matched to a real Mercadona package.
    Whole packages only: you can't buy 300 g of a 1 kg bag.
    Code
  4. 4
    Package prices are summed; leftovers are computed.
    Needed − bought = what's left in your fridge.
    Code
  5. 5
    The total is checked against your budget.
    Within, over, or not realistic — decided by arithmetic, not by the model.
    Code
Whole packages, honest totals

Most planners price 200 g of rice. You buy a 1 kg bag. We price the bag, and show you what's left over so the next plan can use it.
