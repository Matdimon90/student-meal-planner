Method
  1. Soften the chopped onion and pepper in oil, 6 min.
  2. Add lentils and 900 ml water. Simmer 25 min.
  3. Cook the rice separately, 12 min. Serve the lentils over it.
  4. Keep half the lentils for Day 3's soup.
